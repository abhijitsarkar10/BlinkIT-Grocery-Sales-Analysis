# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis 📊

## Project Overview
This project analyzes grocery sales data to identify sales trends, product performance, outlet characteristics, and opportunities for business improvement.

The analysis uses Python and data visualization libraries to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Objectives
- Understand overall grocery sales performance.
- Identify and address data quality issues.
- Analyze sales by product category and outlet type.
- Explore relationships between product characteristics, outlet features, customer ratings, and sales.
- Recommend practical ways to improve business performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The dataset contains 8,523 records and 12 columns covering product information, item visibility, item weight, sales, ratings, and outlet characteristics.

## Project Workflow
1. Data loading and inspection
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Sales analysis by product and outlet
5. Data visualization
6. Business insights and recommendations

## Key Findings
- Fruits and Vegetables is the leading product category by total sales.
- Supermarket Type 1 contributes the largest share of total sales among outlet types.
- Tier 3 locations generate the highest total sales among location tiers.
- Medium-sized outlets have the highest total sales among outlet sizes.
- Missing item weights require appropriate treatment before further analysis.



## Business Value
This analysis supports data-informed decisions about product performance, outlet comparisons, inventory planning, and sales improvement.
